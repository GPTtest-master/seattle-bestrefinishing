## ⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. ❌ Keyword stuffing in business name
2. ❌ Using PO Box or virtual office address for SAB
3. ❌ Creating multiple listings for same business
4. ❌ Ignoring negative reviews
5. ❌ Inconsistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across web
6. ❌ Not posting regularly
7. ❌ Low-quality or stock photos
8. ❌ Leaving Q&A section empty
9. ❌ Not responding to customer messages promptly
10. ❌ Incomplete business information
