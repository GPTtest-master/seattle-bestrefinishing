Our 6-Step Professional Process

STEP 1

Instant Online Quote & Photo Assessment

Submit our 1-minute online form and receive an instant price estimate. Our specialists will contact you quickly to review photos of your bathtub or surfaces. After this quick photo assessment, we provide you with an accurate final quote and confirm the exact price before scheduling.

✓ Fast & Transparent Process:

Fill out the form → Get instant estimate → Send photos via text/email → Receive confirmed final price → Schedule your preferred date and time. No in-home visits required for quotes—everything is done online for your convenience!

STEP 2

Preparing for Your Bath's Transformation

Before our technician arrives, we kindly ask you to clear the bathroom of personal items like towels, shampoo, or even the toothbrushes. If you have a shower screen or plan to change the taps, please do so before the work begins.

✓ What to Expect From Us:

Our skilled technician will arrive in our professional uniform, assess the space, and prepare for proper ventilation. They'll begin the comprehensive refinishing process that's guaranteed to transform your bathroom with our multi-layer system.

STEP 3

Deep Surface Preparation

This is where professionals separate themselves from amateurs. We use industrial-strength cleaning agents to strip away years of soap scum, mineral deposits, body oils, and old coatings. Every chip and crack is meticulously filled and sanded smooth.

⚠️ Why This Matters:

Poor surface prep is the #1 cause of refinishing failure. We spend 40-60% of project time on prep work because proper adhesion determines whether your finish lasts 2 years or 15 years.

✓ Professional Equipment We Use:

Industrial sanders, chemical etching solutions, professional-grade fillers, and HEPA filtration systems for dust control.

STEP 4

Multi-Layer Primer & Topcoat Application

We apply 2-3 coats of commercial-grade bonding primer designed specifically for high-moisture bathroom environments. After proper curing time, we use professional HVLP (High Volume Low Pressure) spray equipment to apply 2-3 layers of premium topcoat. Each coat is meticulously applied with proper drying time between layers to achieve a flawless, glass-like finish.

✓ Premium Materials We Use:

Industrial bonding primers with superior adhesion and anti-microbial additives. Commercial-grade acrylic urethane topcoat (same material used in luxury hotels), UV-resistant pigments that won't yellow over time, and low-VOC eco-friendly formulas safe for your family.

✓ Why Multiple Layers Matter:

Our multi-layer primer creates a waterproof barrier, while multiple topcoat layers add thickness and durability. This 3-5 layer system creates a hard, chemical-resistant surface that withstands daily cleaning products, hot water, and wear for 10-15 years.

STEP 5

Professional Curing & Quality Control

We carefully control ventilation and temperature to ensure optimal curing conditions. Our technicians perform a detailed quality inspection, checking for any imperfections, drips, or missed spots before the coating fully hardens.

✓ Our Quality Standards:

Every surface is inspected under bright lighting from multiple angles. We check for uniform thickness, smooth texture, and flawless appearance. If anything doesn't meet our standards, we fix it immediately.

✓ Complete Cleanup:

We leave your bathroom spotless—removing all equipment, protective coverings, and debris. You'll receive detailed care instructions and warranty documentation.

STEP 6

Flawless Finish Ready in 24 Hours

Your professionally refinished surface is ready to use in just 24 hours. The coating continues to cure and harden over the next week, reaching maximum durability and hardness.

✓ What You Get:

A factory-smooth, glass-like finish that looks and feels brand new. Brilliant, long-lasting color that won't yellow or fade. Easy-to-clean surface that resists stains, mold, and mildew. 5-year warranty backed by our satisfaction guarantee.

✓ Long-Term Durability:

With proper care (detailed instructions provided), your refinished surface will maintain its beautiful appearance for 10-15 years. No messy demolition, no expensive replacement—just professional results at a fraction of the cost.
