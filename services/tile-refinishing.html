Our Tile Refinishing Process

1. Prep & Grout Repair

We clean tile surfaces, remove old caulk, and repair cracked or missing grout. Deep etch for maximum adhesion.

2. Crack & Chip Filling

Any damaged tile is filled with epoxy filler and sanded smooth so the final surface is uniform.

3. Bonding Agent

We apply a professional primer designed for ceramic and porcelain surfaces to ensure the topcoat bonds permanently.

4. Spray Coating

3–4 layers of high-solids acrylic urethane coating are applied with HVLP spray equipment for a smooth, durable finish.

Common Tile Problems We Fix

Cracked or Broken Tile

We fill cracks and repair broken tiles with epoxy before refinishing for a seamless look.

Stained or Discolored Grout

Mold, mildew, and hard water stains are cleaned and sealed. Grout is restored to uniform color.

Outdated Colors

1970s–1990s tile colors (pink, blue, avocado) can be reglazed to modern white or gray.

Porous, Hard-to-Clean Tile

Our coating seals porous tile and grout, making surfaces smooth, non-porous, and easy to clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does tile refinishing last?

With proper care, refinished tile lasts 7–10 years. Use non-abrasive cleaners and avoid harsh scrubbing.

Can you refinish shower floors?

Yes, but we recommend adding a non-slip additive to the coating for safety. We can also apply textured grip coatings.

Will grout lines show?

Yes. Refinishing follows the existing tile pattern. Grout lines are repaired, sealed, and coated for a uniform look.

Is refinishing safe?

Absolutely. We use low-VOC coatings and ensure proper ventilation. Spaces are safe after 12 hours and fully ready after 24 hours.

Can you change tile color?

Yes! We can reglaze tile to any color—most clients choose bright white, off-white, or modern gray.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, we provide a 5-year warranty on workmanship and adhesion under normal use.
