Our Repair Process

1. Assessment & Prep

We inspect damage, clean the area, and remove any rust or loose material. Surface is degreased and etched.

2. Fill & Seal

Industrial-grade epoxy filler is applied in layers, building up to match the original surface contour.

3. Sand & Shape

Repairs are sanded smooth and shaped to blend seamlessly with the surrounding surface.

4. Color Match & Seal

We custom-match the color and apply a protective topcoat for a durable, invisible repair.

Types of Damage We Fix

Chips from Dropped Objects

Shampoo bottles, razors, and tools cause chips. We fill and seal them before water penetrates.

Hairline & Stress Cracks

Thermal stress and age cause cracks. We stabilize and fill them to prevent spreading.

Rust Spots & Corrosion

Water penetration causes rust in cast iron and steel. We treat, seal, and cover rust permanently.

Burns & Scorches

Hot tools and curling irons burn surfaces. We sand, fill, and refinish damaged areas.

Why Repair Instead of Replace?

  • Save 90%+: Replacing a chipped tub costs $3,000–$6,000. Repairing a chip costs $75–$250.
  • Fast service: Most repairs are completed in 1–3 hours. Surfaces are ready to use in 24 hours.
  • Prevent further damage: Small chips become big cracks. Fixing damage early prevents expensive replacements.
  • Invisible repairs: Our color-matching process makes repairs virtually undetectable.
  • Preserve original surfaces: Keep your existing bathtub, tile, or countertop instead of replacing everything.

Common Repair Questions

How long do repairs last?

With proper care, our repairs last 7–10 years or more. We use industrial-grade epoxy that bonds permanently.

Will the repair be visible?

Our color-matching process makes most repairs nearly invisible. Large repairs may have slight texture differences but blend well.

Can you fix deep cracks?

Yes. We stabilize deep cracks with reinforcement and fill them in layers for maximum strength.

Do you repair fiberglass cracks?

Absolutely. Fiberglass cracks are common and repairable with specialized fiberglass resin and reinforcement.

Can you fix rust spots in bathtubs?

Yes. We treat rust with rust converter, fill the damaged area, and seal it to prevent future corrosion.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, we provide a 5-year warranty on all repairs under normal use.

When to Consider Full Refinishing

If you have multiple chips or widespread damage, full refinishing may be more cost-effective than individual repairs. Refinishing covers all damage and updates the entire surface with a fresh coating.

  • 5+ chips or cracks in one fixture
  • Widespread staining or discoloration
  • Outdated colors you want to change
  • Rough, worn surface that needs full renewal
