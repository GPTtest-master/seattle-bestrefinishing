Our Bathtub Refinishing Process

1. Deep Cleaning & Prep

We remove all soap scum, oils, and mineral deposits. The surface is chemically etched to ensure perfect adhesion of the new coating.

2. Chip & Crack Repair

All chips, cracks, and rust spots are filled with industrial-grade epoxy filler and sanded smooth.

3. Primer & Bonding

We apply a professional bonding agent designed specifically for your tub's material—porcelain, cast iron, fiberglass, or acrylic.

4. Multi-Layer Topcoat

3–4 layers of high-solids acrylic urethane coating are sprayed for a smooth, glossy, durable finish. Cures in 24 hours for a true 1‑day turnaround.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does bathtub refinishing last?

With proper care, a professionally refinished tub lasts 7–10 years or more. Use non-abrasive cleaners, avoid bathmats with suction cups, and clean regularly.

Is refinishing safe?

Yes. We use low-VOC, eco-friendly coatings and ensure proper ventilation. The space is safe to enter after 12 hours and fully ready after 24 hours.

Can you refinish fiberglass and acrylic tubs?

Absolutely. We specialize in all materials—fiberglass, acrylic, porcelain, and cast iron. Each requires different prep and coating techniques.

What if my tub has rust or deep cracks?

We repair rust spots and fill cracks with industrial epoxy before refinishing. Most damage can be fixed.

Do you offer warranties?

Yes, we provide a 5-year warranty on workmanship and adhesion. If the coating peels or fails under normal use, we'll re-coat at no charge.

How soon can I use my tub after refinishing?

Light use (hand-washing, no standing water) after 12 hours. Full use (bathing, soaking) after 24 hours once fully cured.
