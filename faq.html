Common Questions About Bathroom Refinishing

Everything you need to know about our refinishing services, warranty coverage, durability, and the process. Click any question to see the answer.

How long before I can use the tub?
+
Typically, the surface is ready for use within 24 hours. We recommend waiting a full 24 hours before using the refinished surface to ensure proper curing. After this time, your bathtub, tile, or shower will be fully functional and ready for regular use.
Is reglazing safe and low-odor?
+
Yes. We use modern, low-VOC materials and professional ventilation to ensure your safety and comfort. Our refinishing process is significantly safer than older methods, with minimal odor that dissipates quickly. We also take care to properly ventilate the work area throughout the process.
How long does it last?
+
If you follow the maintenance instructions provided after the job is complete, our professional coating can last 5–10 years. We use only commercial-grade materials designed for durability and daily use. With proper care—using non-abrasive cleaners and avoiding harsh chemicals—many of our refinished surfaces last even longer than the typical warranty period.
Do you repair chips or rust?
+
Yes — local repairs are part of prep before refinishing. We expertly repair chips, cracks, rust spots, and other surface damage as part of our standard process. Our technicians carefully fill, sand, and prepare these areas so that the final finish is smooth and seamless. This repair work is included in our refinishing service at no additional cost.
What does the warranty cover?
+
Our warranty covers any peeling, bubbling, or workmanship defects that occur during the warranty period — as long as maintenance instructions are followed. If any issue appears, our technician will visit and fix it free of charge under warranty. Damage caused by dropping heavy objects, improper cleaning products, or failure to follow maintenance guidelines is not covered. We provide detailed care instructions after every job to help ensure your refinished surface lasts for years.
Will the bath look brand new after resurfacing?
+
The resurfacing process gives your bath a fresh, rejuvenated appearance and extends its life by many years. While it's not a factory finish, the results are highly effective, cost-efficient, and visually impressive. For cosmetic repairs – we carefully colour match and blend repairs to ensure a seamless look. Most customers are amazed at how dramatically their old, worn bathroom fixtures are transformed to look like new again.
How can I get a quote or schedule a service?
+
Simply fill out our Instant Estimate form or call/text us your project details. You'll receive an instant quote and a manager will contact you to confirm your appointment time and quote. Your final quote will be confirmed by our manager after we receive photos of the surface. The entire process is fast and straightforward—most customers have their quote within minutes and their job scheduled within days.
