Common Surface Problems We Fix

Learn about typical issues affecting bathtubs, showers, sinks, tiles, and countertops — why they happen and how professional refinishing restores like‑new results in just one day.

Chips & Cracks in Bathtubs, Sinks, and Countertops

Chips and cracks are among the most common surface damage issues we encounter in bathroom and kitchen refinishing. These imperfections typically occur from impact damage—dropping heavy objects like shampoo bottles, tools, or soap dishes—or from structural settling in older homes that causes stress fractures in porcelain, fiberglass, and cultured marble surfaces. Left untreated, even small chips can allow water to seep underneath the surface coating, leading to rust stains, further cracking, and eventual coating failure around the damaged area.

Our professional repair process begins with thorough cleaning and rust neutralization if corrosion has started. We use commercial-grade epoxy fillers specifically formulated for bathtub and sink repair, which bond permanently to porcelain, acrylic, and fiberglass substrates. Each repair is meticulously color-matched to your existing surface and sanded smooth before the entire fixture is refinished with our premium coating system. The result is a seamless, durable repair that's backed by our 5-year warranty and indistinguishable from the surrounding surface.

Rust Stains and Corrosion Around Drains

Rust around bathtub and sink drains is a persistent problem caused by standing water, failed caulking, or deteriorating metal drain components. When water sits in contact with steel drain rings or penetrates through cracked caulk, it initiates oxidation that creates unsightly orange-brown stains. These rust stains don't just look bad—they actively corrode the surrounding porcelain or acrylic, creating a progressively larger damaged area that can eventually compromise the structural integrity of the drain seal and lead to leaks beneath your bathroom floor.

Our drain area restoration starts with complete rust neutralization using specialized chemical treatments that stop the oxidation process at the molecular level. We remove all compromised material, rebuild the drain mounting area if necessary, and install fresh caulk seals before applying our refinishing system. The new coating creates a waterproof barrier that prevents future rust formation and restores the drain area to like-new condition. Proper maintenance of caulk seals after refinishing ensures this problem won't recur, giving you decades of rust-free service.

Peeling and Flaking Surface Coatings

Peeling, bubbling, and flaking coatings are telltale signs of improper surface preparation or low-quality refinishing products. This problem commonly occurs with DIY refinishing kits or budget contractors who skip essential preparation steps like acid etching and proper primer application. Without proper surface bonding, any coating—no matter how expensive—will eventually fail as moisture penetrates underneath, causing the finish to lift, bubble, and peel away in sheets. Old factory finishes can also delaminate over time due to age, repeated exposure to harsh cleaning chemicals, or thermal stress from hot water.

Our solution involves complete removal of the failed coating using mechanical stripping and chemical solvents, followed by aggressive acid etching to create microscopic surface texture for superior adhesion. We then apply a two-part epoxy bonding primer formulated specifically for bathtub refinishing, which chemically bonds to the substrate at the molecular level. Our final topcoat system consists of multiple layers of commercial-grade polyurethane or acrylic urethane, cured under controlled conditions to ensure maximum durability. This professional process, backed by our 5-year warranty, prevents the peeling and flaking problems associated with inferior methods and materials.

Surface Discoloration and Yellowing

Yellowing and discoloration in bathtubs, sinks, and countertops stem from multiple sources: UV exposure from bathroom windows, staining from cleaning products containing dyes or bleach, cigarette smoke residue, hard water mineral deposits, and degradation of old refinishing coatings that weren't UV-stabilized. White and light-colored surfaces are particularly susceptible, gradually taking on an unappealing yellow, beige, or gray tint that makes your bathroom look aged and unsanitary despite regular cleaning efforts. This discoloration penetrates into the porous surface material, making it impossible to remove with conventional cleaning methods.

Professional refinishing is the only effective solution for permanent discoloration. Our process begins with deep chemical cleaning to remove surface stains, followed by complete refinishing with UV-stabilized coatings that resist future yellowing. We offer bright white finishes that restore the original factory appearance, or custom color tinting if you prefer almond, biscuit, or other popular bathroom colors. Our commercial-grade topcoats contain UV inhibitors and are formulated to resist staining from cleaning products, hard water, and daily use, ensuring your refinished surface maintains its fresh appearance for 7-10+ years with proper care and maintenance.

Grout Stains, Mildew, and Mold Growth

Stained, discolored grout and mildew growth are chronic problems in tiled showers and bathtub surrounds because standard cement grout is highly porous, absorbing soap scum, body oils, hard water minerals, and moisture deep into its structure. Once these contaminants penetrate the grout, they provide an ideal environment for mold and mildew spores to colonize, creating black, pink, or brown discoloration that's impossible to remove with regular cleaning. Deteriorated caulk seals around tub edges and shower corners also harbor mold growth and allow water infiltration behind tiles, potentially causing hidden water damage to wall structures.

Our tile refinishing process solves this problem permanently by sealing the porous grout surface under a waterproof coating. We begin with thorough decontamination and mildew treatment, remove and replace all failed caulk, and then apply our tile reglazing system that encapsulates both the tiles and grout lines in a seamless, non-porous finish. This eliminates the microscopic crevices where mold grows and creates an easy-to-clean surface that resists future staining and mildew formation. The result is a bright, sanitary shower or tub surround that stays cleaner with minimal maintenance, dramatically reducing the time and effort required for bathroom cleaning while improving indoor air quality by eliminating mold sources.

Worn, Dull, and Scratched Surface Finishes

Daily use, abrasive cleaning products, and accumulated wear gradually degrade bathtub and sink surface finishes, removing the protective gloss layer and creating a dull, rough appearance covered in fine scratches and scuff marks. This wear exposes the underlying substrate to moisture and chemicals, making the surface increasingly difficult to clean and more susceptible to staining. Older porcelain fixtures and previously refinished surfaces are particularly vulnerable, as repeated scrubbing with harsh cleansers or abrasive pads removes microns of coating material year after year until the once-shiny finish becomes porous, rough to the touch, and visually unappealing.

Professional refinishing restores the factory-fresh appearance and protective qualities of worn surfaces. Our process includes thorough mechanical preparation to remove the damaged finish layer, followed by application of high-solids topcoat systems that provide superior durability and chemical resistance. We offer standard high-gloss finishes that create that showroom shine, or optional slip-resistant textured coatings for bathtub floors where safety is a priority. These commercial-grade finishes are formulated to withstand daily use and cleaning products, maintaining their gloss and smoothness far longer than consumer-grade products. With proper care using non-abrasive cleaners, your refinished surface will maintain its beautiful appearance for 7-10+ years.

Hairline Cracks and Surface Crazing

Crazing appears as a network of fine hairline cracks resembling spider webs or cracked pottery glaze, most commonly affecting fiberglass bathtubs, acrylic shower pans, and cultured marble vanity tops. This surface fracturing occurs due to thermal stress from repeated exposure to hot and cold water temperature extremes, age-related brittleness in gelcoat finishes, UV degradation, or flexing of inadequately supported substrates. While crazing typically doesn't compromise the structural integrity of the fixture immediately, these micro-fractures collect dirt, soap residue, and mildew, creating permanently stained lines that are impossible to clean and progressively worsen over time as water infiltration causes additional cracking.

Our approach to crazing repair begins with substrate stabilization—reinforcing the bottom of fiberglass tubs if needed to eliminate flexing that contributes to cracking. We then use specialized filler compounds to penetrate and seal the micro-fractures, followed by thorough surface preparation and refinishing with flexible coating systems designed to accommodate the slight movement inherent in fiberglass and acrylic substrates. For cultured marble surfaces, we can apply decorative stone-look finishes that not only conceal the crazing but also upgrade the aesthetic appearance beyond the original factory finish. Our refinishing process effectively stops crazing progression and restores a smooth, uniform surface with excellent long-term durability.

Burns, Scorch Marks, and Deep Permanent Stains

Severe surface damage from burns (curling irons, dropped cigarettes, hot hair styling tools), chemical etching from drain cleaners or harsh chemicals, and deep penetrating stains from rust rings, hair dye, hard water minerals, and mildew create permanent discoloration that cannot be removed through conventional cleaning or surface polishing. These stains chemically alter or physically damage the surface material itself, etching into porcelain glaze or penetrating deep into porous fiberglass and acrylic substrates where they remain visible regardless of cleaning efforts. Attempting to scrub or bleach out these stains often makes the problem worse by damaging surrounding areas and creating dull spots or additional surface roughness.

Professional refinishing provides complete restoration for even the most severely stained or damaged surfaces. Our technicians assess each problem area individually, using spot repair techniques for localized damage—filling burn marks, neutralizing chemical etching, and priming discolored areas to prevent bleed-through. We then apply full-surface refinishing that creates a uniform, stain-free appearance across the entire fixture. Our coating systems are formulated with stain-blocking primers and highly pigmented topcoats that completely conceal underlying damage and resist future staining from hard water, cleaning products, and daily use. This comprehensive approach, backed by our warranty against re-staining, gives you a fixture that looks brand new and stays that way for years to come.

Care After Refinishing

  • Wait full cure time (we’ll mark on site).
  • Use non‑abrasive cleaners; avoid bleach and dye‑based products.
  • Keep caulk intact; re‑caulk if gaps appear.
  • Use soft mats without suction cups to avoid localized stress.
